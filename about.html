I completed my medical studies at Stanford University, but I am not a licensed physician. I do not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Our Philosophy

We believe in personalized care, second opinions, and the power of biological medicine for better health.

Health Care Beyond Protocols

At Kristine Health Consulting, we understand the importance of getting a second opinion when it comes to your health. When you are facing a critical health decision, it is only natural to want reassurance that you are on the right path. Sometimes the standard protocol in medicine can feel impersonal or inadequate for unique situations. We encourage you to ask for a second opinion: if the medical protocol is sound, there is no harm in confirming it. If, however, you are merely following a standard protocol without considering your individual needs, you may be missing out on more personalized care that could truly benefit you.

Our Belief in Biological Medicine

Unlike conventional treatments that rely heavily on one-size-fits-all protocols, we advocate for the power of biological medicine. This form of treatment focuses on the body's innate ability to heal itself. Biological medicine is rooted in understanding the unique biochemical needs of the individual, and it provides a holistic, tailored approach to healing that is not limited by rigid protocols. We believe that biological medicine is particularly valuable in complex conditions, because it works in harmony with your body's natural processes.

The Limitations of Standard Protocols

Many standard medical treatments are designed to address broad patterns, but they often fail to recognize the nuanced differences between individuals. When you are given a diagnosis, the treatment protocols can overlook the fact that each body responds differently. This is where aspecific therapies, those that do not cater to your personal needs, fall short. Standard protocol treatments may offer temporary relief, but they do not always address the root causes of health issues. We believe that a personalized approach, such as biological medicine, can provide lasting and effective results where standard methods cannot.

Why Choose Us?

We are committed to a healthcare approach that goes beyond the surface. Our goal is not simply to apply a standard treatment protocol to your condition, but to explore all available options, including those in the realm of biological medicine, that align with your unique health profile. We will guide you through every step of the decision-making process, helping you make informed choices that are in your best interest, not just what is easiest or most convenient.

We believe that true health and wellness come from a deep understanding of your body's needs, and we are here to support you in that journey. Whether you are seeking a second opinion or exploring alternatives to conventional therapies, we are ready to help you achieve a healthier, more vibrant life.
