Our Services

Expert support for every step of your healthcare journey

  1. The Second Opinion

    We provide a comprehensive review of your medical case from experienced professionals to explore all possible treatment options.

  2. Medical Escorts

    We accompany patients to their medical appointments and hospital visits, to make sure they receive proper care and have someone to advocate for them.

  3. Hospital Checks and Monitoring

    We visit patients in hospital, verify their condition and ensure they are receiving appropriate care. Our goal is to give patients and families peace of mind by overseeing the care process.

  4. Consultations via Phone or Webcam

    We offer consultations by phone and webcam, so you get flexible support wherever you are: convenient medical advice and guidance tailored to your needs.

  5. Assistance in Finding Hard-to-Find Medications

    We help locate medications that are not easily available, and we assist with the logistics of getting them delivered to you, so you have access to the treatments you need.

  6. Support for Long COVID and Spike Protein Degradation

    We support patients dealing with Long COVID and offer therapies aimed at spike protein degradation, with personalized approaches informed by current medical research.
